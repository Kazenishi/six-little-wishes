Wish 01 of 06

A little something, just for you

Happy birthday, cousin.

Six little wishes. A whole lot of love.

Unwrap your wishes
for you Six little wishes
Scroll for your wishes

Wish 01

A little more magic

May this year surprise you
with a love you can trust,
a dream you can finally touch,
and joy that forgets when to leave.
leave room for something wonderful.

Wish 02

The good kind of trouble

May your cake be generous,
your worries terribly forgetful,
and your luck a little ridiculous.
Here’s to good health, big wins,
and stories we’ll laugh about for years.

something brave, something sweet

Wish 03

Wishes of your own

Before you blow out the candles,
make one wish entirely for yourself.
Something brave, something sweet,
something you’ve almost stopped asking for.
I hope this is the year you get to say,
“It really happened.”

Wish 04

More days like this

I wish you meals that turn into long conversations, good news you can’t wait to share, and mornings when you like where your life is going. May you have the health to enjoy it all and people who celebrate beside you.

whoever you’re becoming

Wish 05

Room for every version of you

Here’s to who you are,
and whoever you’re becoming.
May you try new things,
change your mind,
and find happiness somewhere unexpected.
you have so much living left to enjoy.

Wish 06

Years from now

Someday, we’ll call these the old days.
For now, let’s ask for another slice,
take a slightly embarrassing picture,
and give ourselves something good to remember.
may this year be one you’ll smile about.

And one last thing…
